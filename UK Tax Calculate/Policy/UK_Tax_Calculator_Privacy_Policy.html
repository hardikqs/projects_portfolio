1. Introduction

UK Tax Calculator (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application UK Tax Calculator collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

This app is designed only to help users estimate UK tax and take-home pay calculations. It is intended for calculation purposes only and does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Enter in the App

The app may allow you to enter information such as:

  • Gross salary / income
  • Pay frequency
  • Country or tax-related calculation inputs

This information is used only for tax calculation inside the app. We do not collect, store, or transmit this information to our own servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, address, payment or banking details, or government identification details.

However, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics to help monitor app crashes and improve app performance and stability.

Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostics information
  • Technical performance information related to app stability

3. How We Use Information

  • To calculate UK tax and estimated take-home pay
  • To improve app performance and reliability
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes
  • To maintain and improve app functionality

4. Firebase Crashlytics

Our app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google Firebase, for crash reporting and diagnostics.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention
